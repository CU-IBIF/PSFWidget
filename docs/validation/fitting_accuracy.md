# FWHM measurement accuracy on synthetic beads

Generated by `python tools/fitting_accuracy.py --write`. Do not edit by hand.

**Set-up.** Single 3D Gaussian beads with true FWHM 0.20 µm laterally and 0.60 µm axially, at random sub-voxel positions, point-sampled, with Poisson shot noise on a background of 20 photons per voxel and Gaussian read noise of 2 counts. SNR is the bead's peak above background divided by the background SD, as PSFWidget measures it; the median measured value is shown. Default analysis settings (3×3 line bundles), except that bead-size correction and the SNR and fit-quality acceptance checks are off, so that every bead is fitted. Errors are relative to the true FWHM.

**Per bead**: 500 beads per condition. **Field median**: the median over 10 beads per field, 200 fields per condition. Bias is the mean signed error; |e| the mean absolute error; P95 the 95th percentile of the absolute error.

## Pixel integration

Cameras integrate light over each pixel rather than sampling a point, which widens a Gaussian by √(σ² + p²/12). Rendered with 5×5×5 sub-samples per voxel at the Nyquist limit and SNR 50, the per-bead bias is: x +4.2%, y +4.3%, z +4.1%. This is not corrected yet. From the formula it would be about +1.4% at 4 voxels per FWHM. Point-scanning confocal images are closer to point samples.
